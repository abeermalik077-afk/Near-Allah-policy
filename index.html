Introduction & Core Principles

Welcome to Near Allah ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by abber.innvention. We recognize that prayer is an intimate, spiritual act of worship. As such, Near Allah is purposefully built with a strict privacy-first, on-device architecture.

This Privacy Policy explains the nature of information processed when you use Near Allah on Android, why certain device permissions are requested, how Google AdMob advertisements are served, and your rights concerning your personal data. By installing or using Near Allah, you agree to the practices described herein.

Zero Personal Account Requirement

Near Allah does not require you to create an account, log in with social media, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use the application.

2

Information We Process & How We Use It

Near Allah handles only the minimal technical data strictly necessary to provide its core religious features:

  • Location Data (Coarse and Fine Coordinates):
    To compute accurate daily prayer timings (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and the Qibla orientation, the application needs geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude). You may provide this by:
    1. Selecting a city manually from our pre-compiled catalog (no GPS permission required), or
    2. Granting device location access via GPS for automatic local calculation.
    Crucial Notice: Coordinates are processed entirely on-device through local astronomical algorithms. Location data is stored locally in your device's private preferences and is never uploaded to developer servers, tracked in the background, or sold to third-party data brokers.
  • Daily Prayer Tracking Records & Journal:
    When you check off prayers as completed or record notes in your Namaz journal, this information is stored locally in Android's encrypted private application storage (SharedPreferences). It remains strictly on your device.
  • Camera & Photo Journal Entries:
    Near Allah includes an optional "Scan Namaz" feature that allows you to attach a photograph to your prayer journal record. When you take a photo or select an image from your device gallery, the image file is saved in the app's internal private storage directory (filesDir/prayer_journal_photos). Photos are never uploaded to any cloud service, external server, or shared with third parties.
  • Prayer Calculation & Alarm Settings:
    Preferences such as calculation method (Karachi, Muslim World League, Umm Al-Qura, Egyptian, ISNA), Asr juristic method (Hanafi or Shafi/Standard), and Adhan audio ringtone modes (Makkah Adhan, Madinah Adhan, Tone, or Silent) are stored solely on your local device.

Android Permissions & Hardware Access

To operate reliably on modern Android devices, Near Allah declares specific system permissions in its AndroidManifest.xml. Below is a full, transparent breakdown of each permission and why it is requested:

Permission Category Purpose & App Usage
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Runtime (Optional) Used solely to retrieve your current latitude and longitude to calculate accurate solar prayer timings for your exact location. You may deny this permission and choose a city manually.
CAMERA Runtime (Optional) Used solely if you choose to take a photo of your prayer space or prayer verification for your private personal journal. Declared with android.hardware.camera required="false".
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Allows Near Allah to show notifications when prayer time arrives, including the prayer name and countdown alerts.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM System Alarm Required to schedule exact, timely Adhan reminders at the precise minute of Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha without system battery-saving delays.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Lock Screen UI Allows the full-screen Adhan alert activity to display over the lock screen when a prayer alarm triggers, allowing users to stop or view the alarm immediately.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Audio Playback Used by the prayer alarm service to play the audio Adhan or tone reliably even when the device screen is off or the app is closed.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED System Broadcast Automatically reschedules your daily prayer alarms if your device is restarted or if you travel across timezones.
VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK Device Hardware Provides gentle haptic feedback during alarms and momentarily wakes the CPU to execute scheduled prayer alarm checks.
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Network Required for Google AdMob to retrieve and display advertisements, and to perform initial network checks.

Managing Permissions: You can grant or revoke runtime permissions (Location, Camera, Notifications) at any time through your Android device settings under Settings > Apps > Near Allah > Permissions.

Data Storage, Architecture & Security

We believe the best way to secure user data is not to collect it in the first place. Near Allah implements the following security safeguards:

  • No Backend Server: We do not operate custom cloud servers, remote SQL databases, or central repositories. There is no remote database that could be compromised or leaked.
  • Android App Sandbox: All application data, including your prayer completion calendar and journal photos, is stored within the Android operating system's internal application sandbox. Other applications on your phone cannot access this data without explicit system permissions.
  • Network Security: Any network communication performed by third-party SDKs (Google AdMob) utilizes standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.

User Rights & Data Deletion

Under global data privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Store policies), you have full control over your data. Because all user data is stored locally, you maintain unilateral authority over it at all times:

  • In-App One-Tap Data Deletion:
    You can clear all tracked prayers, calculation settings, and journal records directly inside the app. Open the app, navigate to Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Clear All Saved Data". This immediately purges all saved records from local memory.
  • Deleting Individual Journal Entries:
    You can delete individual prayer journal entries and their associated photos at any time by tapping the trash icon next to any entry on the Profile screen.
  • Uninstalling the Application:
    Uninstalling Near Allah from your Android device permanently and automatically removes all locally stored preferences, prayer records, and journal photos.
